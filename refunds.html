Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. Digital subscriptions

invoicedey Starter and Pro are digital subscription products. In general, once a plan is activated after a successful Paystack payment, charges are non-refundable.

2. When we may refund

  • Duplicate charges for the same plan period caused by a technical error
  • Payment taken but plan not activated within a reasonable time after we confirm the issue
  • Where required by applicable consumer law

3. How to request

Email support@invoicedey.com within 7 days of the charge with:

  • Account email
  • Paystack payment reference
  • Brief description of the issue

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are returned via Paystack to the original payment method and may take several business days to appear.

4. Cancellation

You can stop future renewals anytime under Plan & billing → Cancel renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.
