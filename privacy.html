Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

This policy explains how invoicedey collects and uses personal data when you use our website and app.

1. Data we collect

  • Account details (name, email, business profile)
  • Invoice, client, and payment-record content you enter
  • Billing data needed for Paystack (processed by Paystack; we store plan status and payment references)
  • Technical logs (IP, device, approximate usage) for security and reliability

2. How we use data

  • To provide and improve the Service
  • To process subscriptions and prevent fraud
  • To send transactional messages (e.g. payment confirmation)
  • To respond to support requests

3. Sharing

We use processors such as Supabase (hosting/database/auth) and Paystack (payments). We do not sell your personal data.

4. Retention

We keep account and billing records while your account is active and as required for legal or accounting purposes. You may request deletion of your account by contacting support.

5. Security

We use industry-standard protections (encryption in transit, access controls). No method of transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Contact support@invoicedey.com.
